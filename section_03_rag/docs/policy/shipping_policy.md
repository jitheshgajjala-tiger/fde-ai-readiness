# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
Orders over 50 dollars qualify for free standard shipping.
Customers can cancel an order before dispatch and receive a full refund to the original payment method.
Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled and must be returned under the returns policy.
International orders may be held by customs and delivery times are not guaranteed.
